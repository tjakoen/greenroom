        Roles & logins
        
          
        A role (e.g. admin, student) maps to one or more sites (environments). Give each site the login to use.
        
          
        🔒 Logins are stored only in a private file on this computer (.env). They are never committed, synced, or sent anywhere. Saved usernames are shown so you can see which account is set; passwords are never shown again.
        
          
        
          
        
            
        
              
              
              
            
        
            
        After adding, tick which sites it applies to, then fill in each login.

              Login form (how your app signs in)
              
          Used by login('role') and pre-auth recording so tests can sign in without your app's source. This is the shared form used by every environment; give a single environment a different form with its own Login form section above. Selectors are Playwright locators (e.g. #email); the button is its visible text (e.g. Sign in).
