The local way to use Greenroom: no accounts, no sign-in, everything stays on your computer.

## nav:/run
Pick an environment, leave the role as "Each test's own role" or choose a specific login, then click Run Tests.

## screen
Watch the checklist fill in live. Green is a pass, red is a fail, and the health bar shows how the whole run is going.

## nav:/bug
On any failed check, click Report this. Greenroom packages the trace, video, screenshots, and logs into one file to hand a developer.

## nav:/settings
Settings is where you add your sites and logins. They are saved only on this machine, never uploaded or shared.
