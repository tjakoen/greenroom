The shared way to use Greenroom: sign in once, and your whole team works from one place.

## nav:/environments
Environments is where your team defines the sites to check. Mark one as the default so runs know where to go.

## nav:/accounts
Accounts holds the test logins, stored encrypted. A saved password is never shown again, only that one is set.

## nav:/run
Start a run. Runs are queued and isolated, so several people can run at the same time without stepping on each other.

## nav:/history
History and bug bundles are shared with your team, scoped so people see what they are allowed to see.
