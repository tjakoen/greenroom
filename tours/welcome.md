A quick walk through the app. Nothing here touches your real site. Greenroom runs the checks on your own machine and shows you what happened.

## screen
This is your dashboard. Pick an environment, click Run, and pass or fail shows up right here with screenshots, video, and logs.

## nav:/run
Run is home base. Choose which site to check and which logins to use, then start a run. You can check several sites at once.

## nav:/tests
Tests is your library of checks. Filter by tag, by group, or by what is failing, and run one, some, or all of them.

## nav:/editor
Editor lets you read and tweak a check right in the browser. Every save is kept, so you can always go back to an earlier version.

## nav:/history
History is every past run. Open one to see what passed, what failed, and exactly when.

## nav:/bug
Hit a problem? Report a bug turns a failed run into a single file a developer can open: the trace, the video, the screenshots, and the logs.
